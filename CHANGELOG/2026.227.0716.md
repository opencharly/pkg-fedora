# 2026.227.0716 — docs: adopt the Agent-in-the-Loop Factory tagline

The product positioning line changed. OpenCharly is now **The Agent-in-the-Loop
Factory**, and the tagline sentence is "the fully equipped factory floor for you
and your agents". `opencharly.spec` still described charly by its oldest
wording, "OpenCharly container management CLI", in the two places the RPM
surfaces to a user running `dnf info opencharly`:

- `Summary:` — now "OpenCharly CLI — the fully equipped factory floor for you
  and your agents".
- `%description` — its opening sentence now leads with the same line, followed
  by the unchanged factual body ("Compose, build, deploy, and evaluate boxes
  from a library of fully configurable candies…"). The verb list picked up
  `evaluate` in place of `manage`, matching how the project now describes
  itself; the paragraph's remaining content — the welded-command-plugin note and
  the dnf dependency-resolution note — is untouched apart from re-wrapping.

Description text only. No `Requires:`, `Suggests:`, `%install`, or `%files`
line is touched — the superproject's `charly/packaging_test.go` greps this spec
for dependency parity against the embedded packaging section, and that parity is
unchanged.

Verified with `rpmspec -q --qf '%{SUMMARY}\n%{DESCRIPTION}'`, which parses the
spec (rather than merely grepping it) and returns the new text.
